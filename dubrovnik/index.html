Dubrovnik Old Town Walk

Eight stops, eight short stories, at your own pace. Made for Kim and Dave by Kev.

8Stops
½ mileWalking
23 minListening

How it works

  1. Start at Pile Gate, the main gate where the buses and taxis drop you.
  2. At each stop, tap the big play button. Each story is two to three minutes.
  3. The story ends by telling you where to walk next. Tap Walk here if you want the map.

Tap this on the ship's Wi-Fi before you go ashore. The stories then play even with no signal in town.

Whole route map All stops, one file

Good to know

  • From the ship: most ships dock at Gruž port, about 2½ miles from the Old Town. A taxi, the cruise shuttle or a city bus (lines 1A, 1B, 3 or 8) gets you to Pile Gate in 15 to 20 minutes. If your ship anchors and takes tenders, you land at the Old Port on the far side of town: walk up the main street, the Stradun, to Pile Gate and start there, or play the stops in reverse.
  • Free: the churches, Sponza Palace's courtyard, the market and the stairs. Small tickets: the Franciscan cloister and museum, the Rector's Palace museum, and the cathedral treasury.
  • Optional extra: the City Walls. The entrance is just inside Pile Gate on the left. It's a stunning walk of about 1½ to 2 hours with lots of steps and little shade, so go early if you go.
  • Comfort: the Stradun's polished stone is slippery when wet. Wear shoes with grip, and fill a bottle at Onofrio's Fountain.
  • All aboard: leave at least an hour to get back to the ship.
