The Shape of the Trip

Four days in London, three in Rome, eleven nights cruising the Adriatic and the Aegean, then one last day back on Italian soil at Ostia Antica before flying home — the ancient world bookends this trip both at the start (London through a Roman-and-medieval lens) and at the very end.

Bonus Track

Beat the Jet Lag

Portland to London crosses 8 time zones eastward — one of the harder jet lag scenarios there is. There's an app worth knowing about that actually fixes this with real science, not just "drink water and hope."

What It Is

The app is called Timeshifter. It was built with a Harvard Medical School neuroscientist, Dr. Steven Lockley, who has advised NASA on astronaut sleep schedules since 2010 — the same circadian science NASA uses around rocket launches and spacewalks. Real NASA astronauts have used it and back it. Based on roughly 130,000 post-flight surveys, 96.4% of travelers who followed their plan avoided severe jet lag entirely.

The Idea in Plain English

Jet lag isn't really tiredness — it's your body's internal clock still running on Portland time after your surroundings have moved on. The one thing that resets that clock quickly is light, at the right time. Light in the morning nudges your body clock earlier; light in the evening nudges it later. Get the timing backwards and it can make things worse, which is why generic advice like "just get outside" doesn't reliably work. Eastward trips like this one (Portland to London) are the harder direction, because it's naturally easier for the body to stay up later than to fall asleep earlier.

What the App Actually Tells You

You enter your flight details, usual bedtime, and wake time, and it builds an hour-by-hour plan starting a couple of days before you fly: when to get bright light, when to avoid it, when to sleep or nap, and when to have (or skip) caffeine. It's simple to follow — colored bars and icons on a timeline, not homework.

Download Timeshifter and enter the flight details a few days before Oct 2 — the app recommends starting 2–3 days ahead. First plan is free.

Field Notes & Practicalities

Dinner Ideas by Stop

  • Rome, Torre Argentina areaRoscioli · Armando al Pantheon · Osteria da Fortunata
  • Monti (near the Colosseum)La Taverna dei Fori Imperiali · Trattoria Monti · Ai Tre Scalini
  • Trastevere (near the Vatican)Da Enzo al 29 · Tonnarello · Osteria der Belli
  • Fiumicino (last night)Amelindo · Pascucci al Porticciolo · La Scialuppa da Salvatore

Worth Knowing

  • October in London/Rome runs mild but pack a layer — evenings cool off, especially on the Thames or the Sky Garden viewing deck.
  • Book Sky Garden well ahead (September) — it's free but fills up fast and requires a reservation.
  • The Colosseum booking has a 72-hour cancellation window — worth noting in case plans shift.
  • Windsor Castle is a full day trip by train from Paddington — budget the whole morning.
  • Two sea days on the cruise (Oct 11, Oct 17) are natural rest points between the busier ports.
  • Last full day (Oct 19) is back-to-back: disembark, transfer, Ostia Antica, Necropolis, dinner — a fuller day than it might look on paper.
